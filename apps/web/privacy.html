Privacy

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from third parties.

If you email us, we use your message only to reply and to deliver work you request. We keep it no longer than needed for that and for our legal and accounting obligations, and we do not sell or share it.

The RowProof CLI runs on your machines. It sends requests only to the target you approve, never to us, and it writes results only to files you choose.

For engagements we never ask for production credentials or customer data. Please do not send them.

Questions or deletion requests: contact us.
